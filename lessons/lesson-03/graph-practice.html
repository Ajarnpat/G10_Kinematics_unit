Practising Graph Interpretation and Slope

Four activities to practise reading motion graphs and determining velocity or acceleration from their gradients.

How to use these: Complete each worksheet before opening its worked solutions. Then check your answers, correct mistakes and show your workings with units.
Activity 1 · Straight lines

Position–Time Graphs (A–H)

Describe motion and calculate signed velocity from straight-line gradients.

Activity 2 · Tangents

Curved Position–Time Graphs (A–F)

Velocity–Time Graphs (A–H)
