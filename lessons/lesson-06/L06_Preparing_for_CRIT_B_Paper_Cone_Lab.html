Part 3 · Apply

Thinking ahead to the paper-cone lab

You will later design an investigation of falling paper cones. Use the science you have learned to generate ideas. Do not write a full experimental procedure yet.

Remember: The goal is to bring informed ideas and questions to your Criterion B planning lesson, not to complete your final investigation design in advance.
