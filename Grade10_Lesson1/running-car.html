3 · Try these challenges

Challenge 1 · Same direction

Make the car travel right and speed up. Which way do its velocity and acceleration arrows point?

Challenge 2 · Opposite directions

Make the car initially travel right but slow down. Can you make it stop and reverse?

Challenge 3 · No acceleration

Choose zero acceleration and non-zero velocity. What stays the same?

Challenge 4 · Backwards

Can the car move left while its acceleration points right? Predict what happens.
