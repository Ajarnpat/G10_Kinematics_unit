Describe: At one instant, where is the car, and which way is it moving? Over the whole interval, what can you say about distance, displacement, speed, velocity, average speed, average velocity and acceleration? (Some quantities describe an instant; others describe an interval.)

Discuss the physics

Two important questions

At an instant

Where is the car? How fast is it moving? In which direction? Is its velocity changing at that instant?

Over an interval

How far did it travel? How did its position change? What were its average speed and average velocity?
